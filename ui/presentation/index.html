The Problem in Production Support

Downstream consumers say “balances are wrong”. Support teams must prove if it’s real data issues or expected behavior across RAW, STAGE and MART.

1. Incident Ping-Pong

INC raised → routed to L1 → L2 → engineering. Each team re-writes similar SQL to answer the same “why is this number different?” question.

2. Multi-Stage Confusion

RAW, STAGE and MART tell slightly different stories. No single view of balances, deltas, and transformations along the lineage path.

3. No Self-Service View

Non-SQL users can’t inspect the data directly. They must wait for someone who knows schema, joins, and filters.

Business Value & ROI

DataGuard Copilot is not just a tech PoC — it’s a way to reduce incident cost, improve user experience, and strengthen the client’s competitive position.

1. Direct Incident Cost Reduction

Each INC currently burns L1, L2 and engineering time. By auto-generating lineage views and SQL + narrative, many incidents can be resolved at L1.

  • Fewer handoffs and clarifications.
  • Shorter mean time to resolution (MTTR).
  • More capacity for complex, high-value issues.

2. Long-Term Business Impact & ROI

Over time, the copilot becomes a knowledge asset: incidents, queries and narratives form a reusable catalog of “how we fixed this last time”.

  • Lower onboarding time for new L1 / L2 staff.
  • Consistent investigation patterns across teams.
  • Measurable savings from fewer escalations and rework.

3. Better Client Outcomes

Faster, clearer answers for downstream consumers leads to better service delivery and a stronger competitive position for the client.

  • Business teams get explanations, not just numbers.
  • Improved trust in data products and reports.
  • Capgemini positioned as a partner for intelligent operations.
