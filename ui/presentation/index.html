The Problem

  • Business users & “naive developers” don’t speak SQL or know the schema.
  • Downstream teams raise tickets to upstream data owners just to get basic answers.
  • Days are lost waiting on queries instead of acting on insights.

Symptoms

  • Ticket ping-pong between business, data engineering, and analytics.
  • Context split across graphs, tables, and dashboards.
  • Time-to-answer measured in days, not minutes.

Opportunity

Let business users type questions in natural language (NLP) and “boom”— MCPilot orchestrates graph + SQL, applies governance, and returns trusted answers.

Our Solution

Auto-generate Cypher & SQL from natural language, validate with a semantic model, and visualize results beautifully—all inside this live deck.
